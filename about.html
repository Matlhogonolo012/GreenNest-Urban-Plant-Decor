About GreenNest

​GreenNest is an urban plant decor studio dedicated to bringing nature into city living. We curate modern, low-maintenance plants and stylish planters that complement apartments, offices, and small spaces. From statement Monstera leaves to hardy Snake Plants, we design greenery that fits your lifestyle, not the other way around.


​Whether you’re refreshing a single shelf or transforming your entire living room, we help you choose the right plants, placement, and care plan so your space feels calm, fresh, and effortlessly green.
